# p10_factorial_pow

Find the largest natural number $n$ such that $n! < 3^n$.

Fill in `p10_answer` with a numeric literal and prove the theorem. `IsGreatest S a`
means `a ∈ S` and `a` is an upper bound of `S`; the upper-bound half needs an
argument that works for every larger `n`, not a finite check.
